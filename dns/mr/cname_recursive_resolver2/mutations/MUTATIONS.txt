DNS CNAME Recursive Resolver 2 - Mutation Candidates

Implemented mutations:

BASELINE
  Clean recursive resolver behavior. The resolver forwards the client's
  question upstream and returns the upstream response unchanged.

M1 - REWRITE_QNAME_TO_TARGET
  Before forwarding upstream, rewrite the question name to test.local.
  Expected detection: validateUpstreamRequestEqual reports a qname mismatch.

M2 - REWRITE_QTYPE_AAAA
  Before forwarding upstream, rewrite the question type from A to AAAA.
  Expected detection: validateUpstreamRequestEqual reports a qtype mismatch.

M3 - REWRITE_QCLASS_CH
  Before forwarding upstream, rewrite the question class from IN to CH.
  Expected detection: validateUpstreamRequestEqual reports a qclass mismatch.

M4 - DROP_A_RECORDS
  After receiving the upstream response, remove all A records from answer,
  authority, and additional sections.
  Expected detection: validateResponses reports missing chased A records.

M5 - WRONG_A_RECORDS
  After receiving the upstream response, replace all A records with
  10.10.10.10.
  Expected detection: validateResponses reports different A-record sets.

M6 - MISSING_ONE_A_RECORD
  After receiving the upstream response, keep only the first A record and drop
  any remaining A records.
  Expected detection: validateResponses reports an incomplete A-record set.

M7 - EXTRA_A_RECORD
  After receiving the upstream response, add an extra fake A record
  192.168.1.99.
  Expected detection: validateResponses reports an extra A record.
